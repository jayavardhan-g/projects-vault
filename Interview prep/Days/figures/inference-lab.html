How much memory does the KV cache need?

Every token keeps one K and one V vector per KV head in every layer. Change the model or the budget and watch how many sequences fit.

Mirrors KVCacheConfig.bytes_per_token, bytes_per_block and num_blocks_for_budget in kv_cache.py. Why Llama-3.2-1B and not a Qwen: at 12 KiB/token the pool never fills, so preemption and eviction never fire (README, Model).

Is decode memory-bound or compute-bound at this batch size?

One decode step reads every weight once, plus each sequence's KV cache, and does about 2·P FLOPs per sequence. Slide the batch size and the context length.

Defaults: Llama-3.2-1B, H200, bf16. Attention FLOPs are left out; they are small next to the weight matmuls at these sizes.
